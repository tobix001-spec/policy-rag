# Amendment: Classic Car Collision Deductible Change (May 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective May 27, 2026, the collision deductible for the Classic Car policy changes from $750 to $550. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Classic Car policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Classic Car policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
