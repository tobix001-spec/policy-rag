# Amendment: Premier Auto Bodily Injury Liability Limit Change (July 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective July 09, 2026, the bodily injury liability limit for the Premier Auto policy changes from $500,000 to $750,000. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Premier Auto policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Premier Auto policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
