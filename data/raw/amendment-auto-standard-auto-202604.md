# Amendment: Standard Auto Rental Car Reimbursement Change (April 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective April 08, 2026, the rental car reimbursement for the Standard Auto policy changes from $40 to $48. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Standard Auto policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Standard Auto policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
