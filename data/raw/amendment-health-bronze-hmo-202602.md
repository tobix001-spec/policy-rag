# Amendment: Bronze HMO Annual Deductible Change (February 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective February 22, 2026, the annual deductible for the Bronze HMO plan changes from $500 to $1,000. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Bronze HMO policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Bronze HMO policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
