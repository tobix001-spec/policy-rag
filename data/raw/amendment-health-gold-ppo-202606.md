# Amendment: Gold PPO Coinsurance Rate Change (June 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective June 03, 2026, the coinsurance rate for the Gold PPO plan changes from 25% to 50%. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Gold PPO policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Gold PPO policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
