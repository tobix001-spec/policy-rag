# Amendment: Silver PPO Specialist Visit Copay Change (July 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective July 16, 2026, the specialist visit copay for the Silver PPO plan changes from $75 to $50. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Silver PPO policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Silver PPO policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
