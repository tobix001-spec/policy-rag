# Amendment: HomePlus All-Perils Deductible Change (March 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective March 21, 2026, the all-perils deductible for the HomePlus policy changes from $2,500 to $1,500. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the HomePlus policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All HomePlus policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
