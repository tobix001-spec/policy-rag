# Amendment: Landlord Protect Additional Living Expense Period Change (May 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective May 15, 2026, the additional living expense period for the Landlord Protect policy changes from 24 months to 30 months. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Landlord Protect policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Landlord Protect policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
