# Amendment: Renters Essential All-Perils Deductible Change (June 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective June 26, 2026, the all-perils deductible for the Renters Essential policy changes from $1,500 to $3,000. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Renters Essential policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Renters Essential policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
