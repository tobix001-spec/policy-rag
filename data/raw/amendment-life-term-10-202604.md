# Amendment: Term 10 Free-Look Period Change (April 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective April 26, 2026, the free-look period for the Term 10 policy changes from 10 days to 12 days. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Term 10 policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Term 10 policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
