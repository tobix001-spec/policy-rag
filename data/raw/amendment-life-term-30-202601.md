# Amendment: Term 30 Maximum Issue Age Change (January 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective January 16, 2026, the maximum issue age for the Term 30 policy changes from 65 years to 39 years. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Term 30 policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Term 30 policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
