# Amendment: Universal Life Maximum Face Amount Change (July 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective July 01, 2026, the maximum face amount for the Universal Life policy changes from $5,000,000 to $7,500,000. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Universal Life policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Universal Life policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
