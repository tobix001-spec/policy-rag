# Amendment: Multi-Pet Reimbursement Rate Change (January 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective January 10, 2026, the reimbursement rate for the Multi-Pet plan changes from 60% to 120%. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Multi-Pet policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Multi-Pet policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
