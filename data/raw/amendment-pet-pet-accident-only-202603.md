# Amendment: Pet Accident Only Illness Waiting Period Change (March 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective March 12, 2026, the illness waiting period for the Pet Accident Only plan changes from 15 days to 11 days. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Pet Accident Only policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Pet Accident Only policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
