# Amendment: Pet Wellness Maximum Enrollment Age Change (June 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective June 10, 2026, the maximum enrollment age for the Pet Wellness plan changes from 14 years to 11 years. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Pet Wellness policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Pet Wellness policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
