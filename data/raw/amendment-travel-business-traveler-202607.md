# Amendment: Business Traveler Baggage Delay Waiting Period Change (July 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective July 18, 2026, the baggage delay waiting period for the Business Traveler plan changes from 12 hours to 15 hours. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Business Traveler policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Business Traveler policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
