# Amendment: Trip Premier Baggage Delay Waiting Period Change (July 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective July 06, 2026, the baggage delay waiting period for the Trip Premier plan changes from 6 hours to 12 hours. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Trip Premier policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Trip Premier policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
