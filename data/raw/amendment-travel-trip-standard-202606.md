# Amendment: Trip Standard Medical Evacuation Limit Change (June 2026)

## Summary of Change

Effective June 04, 2026, the medical evacuation limit for the Trip Standard plan changes from $500,000 to $375,000. This amendment supersedes the corresponding provision of the Trip Standard policy document. All other terms remain unchanged.

## Who Is Affected

All Trip Standard policies issued or renewed on or after the effective date.
