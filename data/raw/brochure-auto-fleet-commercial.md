## Why Choose Fleet Commercial

Fleet Commercial is designed for customers who want dependable auto coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Rental reimbursement on Fleet Commercial is capped at $35 per day. The Fleet Commercial policy provides bodily injury liability coverage of $250,000 per person.
