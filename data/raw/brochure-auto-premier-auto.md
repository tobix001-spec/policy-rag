# Premier Auto at a Glance

## Why Choose Premier Auto

Premier Auto is designed for customers who want dependable auto coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Roadside assistance on Premier Auto is limited to 6 service calls each policy term. Rental reimbursement on Premier Auto is capped at $30 per day.
