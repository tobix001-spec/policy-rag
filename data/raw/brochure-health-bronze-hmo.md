# Bronze HMO at a Glance

## Why Choose Bronze HMO

Bronze HMO is designed for customers who want dependable health coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Bronze HMO members pay $300 for each emergency room visit unless admitted. After the deductible, Bronze HMO members pay 30% coinsurance for covered in-network services.
