## Why Choose Catastrophic EPO

Catastrophic EPO is designed for customers who want dependable health coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

The annual deductible for the Catastrophic EPO plan is $1,500 per individual. The Catastrophic EPO emergency room copay is $350 per visit; it is waived on inpatient admission.
