# Gold PPO at a Glance

## Why Choose Gold PPO

Gold PPO is designed for customers who want dependable health coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

A $30 copay applies to each in-network specialist office visit under Gold PPO. The Gold PPO emergency room copay is $250 per visit; it is waived on inpatient admission.
