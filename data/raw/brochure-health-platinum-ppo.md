# Platinum PPO at a Glance

## Why Choose Platinum PPO

Platinum PPO is designed for customers who want dependable health coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

After the deductible, Platinum PPO members pay 15% coinsurance for covered in-network services. In-network specialist visits on Platinum PPO have a $65 copay.
