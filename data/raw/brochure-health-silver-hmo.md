# Silver HMO at a Glance

## Why Choose Silver HMO

Silver HMO is designed for customers who want dependable health coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Silver HMO carries a $250 individual deductible each plan year. Once a member's cost sharing reaches $8,200, Silver HMO pays 100% of covered in-network services for the rest of the year.
