# Silver PPO at a Glance

## Why Choose Silver PPO

Silver PPO is designed for customers who want dependable health coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

In-network specialist visits on Silver PPO have a $75 copay. Members enrolled in Silver PPO must meet an individual annual deductible of $2,000 before coinsurance applies.
