# HomeBasic at a Glance

## Why Choose HomeBasic

HomeBasic is designed for customers who want dependable home coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

HomeBasic covers hotel and temporary housing costs for a maximum of 9 months. Sewer and drain backup losses on HomeBasic are limited to $25,000.
