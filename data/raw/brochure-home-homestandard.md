# HomeStandard at a Glance

## Why Choose HomeStandard

HomeStandard is designed for customers who want dependable home coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Each property claim on HomeStandard is subject to a $5,000 deductible. If your home becomes uninhabitable, HomeStandard pays additional living expenses for up to 12 months.
