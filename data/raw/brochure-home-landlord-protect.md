# Landlord Protect at a Glance

## Why Choose Landlord Protect

Landlord Protect is designed for customers who want dependable home coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Landlord Protect applies a $5,000 special limit to jewelry theft. If your home becomes uninhabitable, Landlord Protect pays additional living expenses for up to 24 months.
