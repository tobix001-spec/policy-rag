# Multi-Pet at a Glance

## Why Choose Multi-Pet

Multi-Pet is designed for customers who want dependable pet coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Illness coverage under Multi-Pet begins after a 10 days waiting period. Once the deductible is met, Multi-Pet pays back 60% of covered vet bills.
