# Senior Pet at a Glance

## Why Choose Senior Pet

Senior Pet is designed for customers who want dependable pet coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Once the deductible is met, Senior Pet pays back 75% of covered vet bills. Pets can be newly enrolled in Senior Pet up to 12 years of age.
