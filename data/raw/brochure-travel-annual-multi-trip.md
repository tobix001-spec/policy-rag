# Annual Multi-Trip at a Glance

## Why Choose Annual Multi-Trip

Annual Multi-Trip is designed for customers who want dependable travel coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Annual Multi-Trip reimburses essential purchases after a baggage delay of 24 hours. Trip cancellation benefits under Annual Multi-Trip reimburse prepaid, non-refundable costs up to $25,000 per trip.
