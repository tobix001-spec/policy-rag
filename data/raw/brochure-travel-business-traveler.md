# Business Traveler at a Glance

## Why Choose Business Traveler

Business Traveler is designed for customers who want dependable travel coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

Business Traveler allows the cancel-for-any-reason upgrade only within 15 days of the initial deposit. Baggage delay benefits on Business Traveler begin once checked luggage is delayed more than 12 hours.
