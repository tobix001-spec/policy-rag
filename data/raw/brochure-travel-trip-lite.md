# Trip Lite at a Glance

## Why Choose Trip Lite

Trip Lite is designed for customers who want dependable travel coverage backed by Velmora Assurance's award-winning service.

## Highlights

The cancel-for-any-reason upgrade for Trip Lite must be purchased within 7 days of the initial trip deposit. Emergency medical evacuation is covered up to $750,000 under Trip Lite.
