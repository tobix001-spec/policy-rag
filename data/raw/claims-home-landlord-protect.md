# Velmora Assurance Landlord Protect — Claims Guide

## How to File a Claim

For Landlord Protect, the filing deadline is 120 days after the loss occurs; late claims may be denied.

Claims can be started in the online portal, in the mobile app, or by calling the claims line. You will receive a claim number by email within one business day.

## Required Documents

Please provide the following:

- photos or video of damaged property
- list of damaged items with estimated values
- receipts if available
- contractor repair estimate

## Processing Timeline

Once all documents are received, a Landlord Protect claim decision is made within 45 days.

If we need more information, the timeline pauses until it is received.

## Appeals

Appeals for Landlord Protect must be received within 60 days after you receive the explanation of benefits.

Appeals are reviewed by a person who was not involved in the original decision.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
