# Velmora Assurance Business Traveler — Claims Guide

## How to File a Claim

Claims under the Business Traveler plan must be submitted within 180 days of the date of loss or service.

Claims can be started in the online portal, in the mobile app, or by calling the claims line. You will receive a claim number by email within one business day.

## Required Documents

Please provide the following:

- proof of travel dates
- receipts for prepaid costs
- carrier delay confirmation
- medical records for illness claims

## Processing Timeline

Velmora Assurance issues a decision on complete Business Traveler claims within 10 days of receipt.

If we need more information, the timeline pauses until it is received.

## Appeals

If a Business Traveler claim is denied, you may file a written appeal within 120 days of the denial notice.

Appeals are reviewed by a person who was not involved in the original decision.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
