# INTERNAL — Auto Adjuster Guidelines

## Purpose

For claims staff only. Do not share with customers.

## Referral Rules

Auto claims with an estimated loss above $50,000 must be referred to a senior adjuster.

Refer a auto policyholder to the Special Investigations Unit after 5 claims within any 12-month period.
