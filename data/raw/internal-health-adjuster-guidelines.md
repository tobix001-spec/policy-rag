# INTERNAL — Health Adjuster Guidelines

## Purpose

For claims staff only. Do not share with customers.

## Referral Rules

Health claims with an estimated loss above $50,000 must be referred to a senior adjuster.

Refer a health policyholder to the Special Investigations Unit after 3 claims within any 12-month period.
