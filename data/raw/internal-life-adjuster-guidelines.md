# INTERNAL — Life Adjuster Guidelines

## Purpose

For claims staff only. Do not share with customers.

## Referral Rules

Life claims with an estimated loss above $75,000 must be referred to a senior adjuster.

Refer a life policyholder to the Special Investigations Unit after 2 claims within any 12-month period.
