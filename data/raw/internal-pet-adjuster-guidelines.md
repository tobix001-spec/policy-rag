# INTERNAL — Pet Adjuster Guidelines

## Purpose

For claims staff only. Do not share with customers.

## Referral Rules

Pet claims with an estimated loss above $15,000 must be referred to a senior adjuster.

Refer a pet policyholder to the Special Investigations Unit after 7 claims within any 12-month period.
