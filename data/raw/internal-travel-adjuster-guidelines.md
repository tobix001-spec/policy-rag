# INTERNAL — Travel Adjuster Guidelines

## Purpose

For claims staff only. Do not share with customers.

## Referral Rules

Travel claims with an estimated loss above $25,000 must be referred to a senior adjuster.

Refer a travel policyholder to the Special Investigations Unit after 6 claims within any 12-month period.
