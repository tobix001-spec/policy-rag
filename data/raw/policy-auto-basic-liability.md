# Velmora Assurance Basic Liability Auto Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Basic Liability auto policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Physical Damage Coverage

For collision claims, Basic Liability applies a $250 deductible. Basic Liability applies a $1,250 deductible to comprehensive (non-collision) claims. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Optional Coverages

Roadside assistance on Basic Liability is limited to 3 service calls each policy term. Rental reimbursement on Basic Liability is capped at $75 per day. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Liability Coverage

Basic Liability pays up to $25,000 per injured person for bodily injury liability. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Exclusions

The Basic Liability policy does not cover:

- wear and tear
- using the vehicle for delivery without an endorsement
- racing or speed contests
- drivers excluded by name

## General Terms

A grace period of 20 days applies to late premium payments on the Basic Liability policy. Before cancelling your Basic Liability coverage, we send written notice at least 10 days in advance.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Definitions and Conditions

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
