# Velmora Assurance Classic Car Auto Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Classic Car auto policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Physical Damage Coverage

The comprehensive deductible for Classic Car is $850. Collision losses under the Classic Car policy are subject to a $750 deductible. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Optional Coverages

While a covered vehicle is in the shop, Classic Car pays up to $50 per day toward a rental. Policyholders on Classic Car may use 2 service calls of roadside assistance per term. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Liability Coverage

The Classic Car policy provides bodily injury liability coverage of $100,000 per person. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Exclusions

The Classic Car policy does not cover:

- mechanical breakdown
- using the vehicle for delivery without an endorsement
- wear and tear
- racing or speed contests

## General Terms

Classic Car policyholders receive a 10 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. The minimum cancellation notice for Classic Car is 30 days.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Definitions and Conditions

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
