# Velmora Assurance Fleet Commercial Auto Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Fleet Commercial auto policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Physical Damage Coverage

The Fleet Commercial collision deductible is $1,000 per incident. Comprehensive losses such as theft, hail and vandalism carry a $100 deductible on Fleet Commercial. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Optional Coverages

While a covered vehicle is in the shop, Fleet Commercial pays up to $35 per day toward a rental. Policyholders on Fleet Commercial may use 4 service calls of roadside assistance per term. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Liability Coverage

Fleet Commercial pays up to $250,000 per injured person for bodily injury liability. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Exclusions

The Fleet Commercial policy does not cover:

- intentional damage
- using the vehicle for delivery without an endorsement
- mechanical breakdown
- custom equipment over the scheduled limit

## General Terms

If a Fleet Commercial premium is missed, coverage continues during a 60 days grace period. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 60 days written notice before cancelling a Fleet Commercial policy.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Definitions and Conditions

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
