# Velmora Assurance Premier Auto Auto Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Premier Auto auto policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Physical Damage Coverage

The Premier Auto collision deductible is $2,000 per incident. The comprehensive deductible for Premier Auto is $250. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Optional Coverages

Policyholders on Premier Auto may use 6 service calls of roadside assistance per term. While a covered vehicle is in the shop, Premier Auto pays up to $30 per day toward a rental. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Liability Coverage

The Premier Auto policy provides bodily injury liability coverage of $500,000 per person. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The Premier Auto policy does not cover:

- drivers excluded by name
- using the vehicle for delivery without an endorsement
- intentional damage
- racing or speed contests

## General Terms

If a Premier Auto premium is missed, coverage continues during a 45 days grace period. Before cancelling your Premier Auto coverage, we send written notice at least 45 days in advance.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Definitions and Conditions

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
