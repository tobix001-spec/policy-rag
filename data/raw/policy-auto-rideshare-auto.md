# Velmora Assurance Rideshare Auto Auto Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Rideshare Auto auto policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Physical Damage Coverage

Comprehensive losses such as theft, hail and vandalism carry a $600 deductible on Rideshare Auto. The Rideshare Auto collision deductible is $1,500 per incident. Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Optional Coverages

Policyholders on Rideshare Auto may use 8 service calls of roadside assistance per term. While a covered vehicle is in the shop, Rideshare Auto pays up to $60 per day toward a rental. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Liability Coverage

Bodily injury liability under Rideshare Auto is limited to $300,000 per person. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The Rideshare Auto policy does not cover:

- intentional damage
- using the vehicle for delivery without an endorsement
- racing or speed contests
- drivers excluded by name

## General Terms

If a Rideshare Auto premium is missed, coverage continues during a 30 days grace period. The minimum cancellation notice for Rideshare Auto is 20 days.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Definitions and Conditions

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
