# Velmora Assurance Standard Auto Auto Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Standard Auto auto policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Physical Damage Coverage

For collision claims, Standard Auto applies a $500 deductible. Comprehensive losses such as theft, hail and vandalism carry a $400 deductible on Standard Auto. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Optional Coverages

Roadside assistance on Standard Auto is limited to 5 service calls each policy term. Standard Auto reimburses rental cars up to $40 per day while your vehicle is being repaired after a covered loss. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Liability Coverage

The Standard Auto policy provides bodily injury liability coverage of $50,000 per person. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Exclusions

The Standard Auto policy does not cover:

- wear and tear
- drivers excluded by name
- using the vehicle for delivery without an endorsement
- mechanical breakdown

## General Terms

Standard Auto policyholders receive a 15 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. The minimum cancellation notice for Standard Auto is 14 days.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
