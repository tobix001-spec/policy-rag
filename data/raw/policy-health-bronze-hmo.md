# Velmora Assurance Bronze HMO Health Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Bronze HMO health plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Cost Sharing

After the deductible, Bronze HMO members pay 30% coinsurance for covered in-network services. Bronze HMO carries a $500 individual deductible each plan year. The individual out-of-pocket maximum under Bronze HMO is $4,000 per plan year. The Bronze HMO emergency room copay is $300 per visit; it is waived on inpatient admission. In-network specialist visits on Bronze HMO have a $20 copay. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Exclusions

The Bronze HMO plan does not cover:

- experimental or investigational treatments
- hearing aids
- weight-loss programs
- services received outside the United States except emergencies

## General Terms

A grace period of 60 days applies to late premium payments on the Bronze HMO plan. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 60 days written notice before cancelling a Bronze HMO plan.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Definitions and Conditions

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
