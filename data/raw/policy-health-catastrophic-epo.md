# Velmora Assurance Catastrophic EPO Health Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Catastrophic EPO health plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Cost Sharing

After the deductible, Catastrophic EPO members pay 40% coinsurance for covered in-network services. In-network specialist visits on Catastrophic EPO have a $40 copay. The annual deductible for the Catastrophic EPO plan is $1,500 per individual. The individual out-of-pocket maximum under Catastrophic EPO is $9,100 per plan year. Emergency room visits under Catastrophic EPO carry a $350 copay, waived if you are admitted. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Exclusions

The Catastrophic EPO plan does not cover:

- experimental or investigational treatments
- routine dental care for adults
- weight-loss programs
- fertility treatment

## General Terms

A grace period of 10 days applies to late premium payments on the Catastrophic EPO plan. The minimum cancellation notice for Catastrophic EPO is 14 days.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Definitions and Conditions

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
