# Velmora Assurance Gold PPO Health Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Gold PPO health plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Cost Sharing

Once a member's cost sharing reaches $7,500, Gold PPO pays 100% of covered in-network services for the rest of the year. In-network specialist visits on Gold PPO have a $30 copay. The member coinsurance on Gold PPO is 25% after the deductible. Emergency room visits under Gold PPO carry a $250 copay, waived if you are admitted. Gold PPO carries a $750 individual deductible each plan year. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Exclusions

The Gold PPO plan does not cover:

- hearing aids
- weight-loss programs
- cosmetic surgery
- services received outside the United States except emergencies

## General Terms

Gold PPO policyholders receive a 20 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. Before cancelling your Gold PPO coverage, we send written notice at least 20 days in advance.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
