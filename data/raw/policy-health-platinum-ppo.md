# Velmora Assurance Platinum PPO Health Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Platinum PPO health plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Cost Sharing

Platinum PPO carries a $6,000 individual deductible each plan year. The Platinum PPO emergency room copay is $100 per visit; it is waived on inpatient admission. The individual out-of-pocket maximum under Platinum PPO is $5,500 per plan year. In-network specialist visits on Platinum PPO have a $65 copay. The member coinsurance on Platinum PPO is 15% after the deductible. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Exclusions

The Platinum PPO plan does not cover:

- fertility treatment
- hearing aids
- experimental or investigational treatments
- long-term custodial care

## General Terms

If a Platinum PPO premium is missed, coverage continues during a 30 days grace period. The minimum cancellation notice for Platinum PPO is 30 days.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
