# Velmora Assurance Silver HMO Health Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Silver HMO health plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Cost Sharing

Silver HMO members pay $150 for each emergency room visit unless admitted. The annual deductible for the Silver HMO plan is $250 per individual. In-network specialist visits on Silver HMO have a $90 copay. After the deductible, Silver HMO members pay 20% coinsurance for covered in-network services. Silver HMO limits individual out-of-pocket spending to $8,200 annually. We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Exclusions

The Silver HMO plan does not cover:

- hearing aids
- routine dental care for adults
- long-term custodial care
- weight-loss programs

## General Terms

Silver HMO policyholders receive a 45 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. The minimum cancellation notice for Silver HMO is 10 days.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Definitions and Conditions

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
