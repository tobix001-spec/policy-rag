# Velmora Assurance Silver PPO Health Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Silver PPO health plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Cost Sharing

The Silver PPO emergency room copay is $500 per visit; it is waived on inpatient admission. In-network specialist visits on Silver PPO have a $75 copay. Silver PPO carries a $2,000 individual deductible each plan year. After the deductible, Silver PPO members pay 10% coinsurance for covered in-network services. Silver PPO limits individual out-of-pocket spending to $3,000 annually. Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Exclusions

The Silver PPO plan does not cover:

- routine dental care for adults
- services received outside the United States except emergencies
- fertility treatment
- cosmetic surgery

## General Terms

If a Silver PPO premium is missed, coverage continues during a 31 days grace period. Before cancelling your Silver PPO coverage, we send written notice at least 45 days in advance.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Definitions and Conditions

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
