# Velmora Assurance Condo Shield Home Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Condo Shield home policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Deductibles

The Condo Shield policy carries a $1,000 deductible for covered property losses. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Special Limits

Condo Shield applies a $10,000 special limit to jewelry theft. Water backup of sewers or drains is covered up to $7,500 under Condo Shield. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Loss of Use

If your home becomes uninhabitable, Condo Shield pays additional living expenses for up to 3 months. No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Exclusions

The Condo Shield policy does not cover:

- mold resulting from long-term leaks
- earthquake
- flood
- home business property over $2,500

## General Terms

A grace period of 20 days applies to late premium payments on the Condo Shield policy. Before cancelling your Condo Shield coverage, we send written notice at least 60 days in advance.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Definitions and Conditions

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
