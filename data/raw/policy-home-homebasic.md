# Velmora Assurance HomeBasic Home Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the HomeBasic home policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Deductibles

Each property claim on HomeBasic is subject to a $2,000 deductible. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Special Limits

Theft of jewelry and watches is limited to $7,500 under HomeBasic unless scheduled. Water backup of sewers or drains is covered up to $25,000 under HomeBasic. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Loss of Use

HomeBasic covers hotel and temporary housing costs for a maximum of 9 months. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Exclusions

The HomeBasic policy does not cover:

- flood
- mold resulting from long-term leaks
- earthquake
- pest infestation

## General Terms

HomeBasic policyholders receive a 60 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 45 days written notice before cancelling a HomeBasic policy.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Definitions and Conditions

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
