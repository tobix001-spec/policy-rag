# Velmora Assurance HomePlus Home Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the HomePlus home policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Deductibles

An all-perils deductible of $2,500 applies to each covered loss under HomePlus. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Special Limits

Water backup of sewers or drains is covered up to $10,000 under HomePlus. HomePlus applies a $1,500 special limit to jewelry theft. No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Loss of Use

HomePlus covers hotel and temporary housing costs for a maximum of 6 months. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The HomePlus policy does not cover:

- earthquake
- flood
- mold resulting from long-term leaks
- pest infestation

## General Terms

A grace period of 15 days applies to late premium payments on the HomePlus policy. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 20 days written notice before cancelling a HomePlus policy.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Definitions and Conditions

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
