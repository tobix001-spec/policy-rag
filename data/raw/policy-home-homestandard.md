# Velmora Assurance HomeStandard Home Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the HomeStandard home policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Deductibles

The HomeStandard policy carries a $5,000 deductible for covered property losses. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Special Limits

HomeStandard applies a $2,000 special limit to jewelry theft. Sewer and drain backup losses on HomeStandard are limited to $5,000. Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Loss of Use

Additional living expense coverage on HomeStandard lasts up to 12 months. We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Exclusions

The HomeStandard policy does not cover:

- intentional acts
- pest infestation
- mold resulting from long-term leaks
- home business property over $2,500

## General Terms

A grace period of 31 days applies to late premium payments on the HomeStandard policy. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 14 days written notice before cancelling a HomeStandard policy.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
