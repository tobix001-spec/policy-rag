# Velmora Assurance Landlord Protect Home Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Landlord Protect home policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Deductibles

The Landlord Protect policy carries a $500 deductible for covered property losses. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Special Limits

Landlord Protect applies a $5,000 special limit to jewelry theft. Sewer and drain backup losses on Landlord Protect are limited to $20,000. We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Loss of Use

Landlord Protect covers hotel and temporary housing costs for a maximum of 24 months. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Exclusions

The Landlord Protect policy does not cover:

- vacancy beyond 60 consecutive days
- intentional acts
- mold resulting from long-term leaks
- flood

## General Terms

A grace period of 30 days applies to late premium payments on the Landlord Protect policy. Before cancelling your Landlord Protect coverage, we send written notice at least 10 days in advance.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Definitions and Conditions

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
