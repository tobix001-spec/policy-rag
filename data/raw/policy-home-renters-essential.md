# Velmora Assurance Renters Essential Home Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Renters Essential home policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Deductibles

An all-perils deductible of $1,500 applies to each covered loss under Renters Essential. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Special Limits

Unscheduled jewelry is covered for theft up to $2,500 on Renters Essential. Water backup of sewers or drains is covered up to $15,000 under Renters Essential. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Loss of Use

If your home becomes uninhabitable, Renters Essential pays additional living expenses for up to 18 months. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Exclusions

The Renters Essential policy does not cover:

- intentional acts
- earthquake
- mold resulting from long-term leaks
- flood

## General Terms

A grace period of 45 days applies to late premium payments on the Renters Essential policy. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 30 days written notice before cancelling a Renters Essential policy.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Definitions and Conditions

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
