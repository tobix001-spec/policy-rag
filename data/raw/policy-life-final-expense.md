# Velmora Assurance Final Expense Life Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Final Expense life policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Policyholder Rights

You may cancel Final Expense for a full refund within 15 days of delivery (the free-look period). Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Coverage Amounts

The maximum face amount available on Final Expense is $1,000,000. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Eligibility

The maximum issue age for Final Expense is 85 years. No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Exclusions

The Final Expense policy does not cover:

- suicide within the first two policy years
- acts of war
- death while committing a felony
- material misrepresentation on the application

## General Terms

If a Final Expense premium is missed, coverage continues during a 45 days grace period. The minimum cancellation notice for Final Expense is 14 days.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Definitions and Conditions

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
