# Velmora Assurance Term 10 Life Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Term 10 life policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Policyholder Rights

You may cancel Term 10 for a full refund within 10 days of delivery (the free-look period). If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Coverage Amounts

Term 10 may be issued with a face amount of up to $50,000. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Eligibility

The maximum issue age for Term 10 is 70 years. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The Term 10 policy does not cover:

- suicide within the first two policy years
- material misrepresentation on the application
- acts of war
- death while committing a felony

## General Terms

Term 10 policyholders receive a 20 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. The minimum cancellation notice for Term 10 is 30 days.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Definitions and Conditions

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
