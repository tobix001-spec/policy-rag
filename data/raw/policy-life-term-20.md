# Velmora Assurance Term 20 Life Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Term 20 life policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Policyholder Rights

You may cancel Term 20 for a full refund within 31 days of delivery (the free-look period). Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Coverage Amounts

The maximum face amount available on Term 20 is $2,000,000. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Eligibility

The maximum issue age for Term 20 is 75 years. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The Term 20 policy does not cover:

- material misrepresentation on the application
- death while committing a felony
- acts of war
- suicide within the first two policy years

## General Terms

Term 20 policyholders receive a 10 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. The minimum cancellation notice for Term 20 is 45 days.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Definitions and Conditions

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
