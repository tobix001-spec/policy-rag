# Velmora Assurance Term 30 Life Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Term 30 life policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Policyholder Rights

Term 30 provides a 45 days free-look period for a full premium refund. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Coverage Amounts

Term 30 may be issued with a face amount of up to $250,000. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Eligibility

The maximum issue age for Term 30 is 65 years. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Exclusions

The Term 30 policy does not cover:

- death while committing a felony
- material misrepresentation on the application
- acts of war
- suicide within the first two policy years

## General Terms

A grace period of 15 days applies to late premium payments on the Term 30 policy. The minimum cancellation notice for Term 30 is 20 days.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Definitions and Conditions

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
