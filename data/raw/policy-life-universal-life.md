# Velmora Assurance Universal Life Life Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Universal Life life policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Policyholder Rights

You may cancel Universal Life for a full refund within 30 days of delivery (the free-look period). Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Coverage Amounts

The maximum face amount available on Universal Life is $5,000,000. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be no older than 55 years to purchase Universal Life. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Exclusions

The Universal Life policy does not cover:

- acts of war
- material misrepresentation on the application
- suicide within the first two policy years
- death while committing a felony

## General Terms

A grace period of 31 days applies to late premium payments on the Universal Life policy. The minimum cancellation notice for Universal Life is 10 days.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Definitions and Conditions

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
