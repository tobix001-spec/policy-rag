# Velmora Assurance Whole Life Life Policy — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Whole Life life policy offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Policyholder Rights

You may cancel Whole Life for a full refund within 20 days of delivery (the free-look period). We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Coverage Amounts

Coverage under Whole Life can be purchased up to $500,000. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Eligibility

Whole Life can be issued to applicants up to 60 years of age. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Exclusions

The Whole Life policy does not cover:

- acts of war
- death while committing a felony
- material misrepresentation on the application
- suicide within the first two policy years

## General Terms

A grace period of 60 days applies to late premium payments on the Whole Life policy. The minimum cancellation notice for Whole Life is 60 days.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Definitions and Conditions

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
