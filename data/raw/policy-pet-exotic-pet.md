# Velmora Assurance Exotic Pet Pet Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Exotic Pet pet plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Benefits

The annual benefit limit for Exotic Pet is $3,000. The reimbursement rate on Exotic Pet is 80%. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Waiting Periods

New Exotic Pet policies have a 14 days illness waiting period. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Eligibility

Exotic Pet accepts new enrollments for pets up to 9 years old. Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Exclusions

The Exotic Pet plan does not cover:

- cosmetic procedures such as tail docking
- breeding and pregnancy costs
- grooming
- food and supplements unless prescribed

## General Terms

A grace period of 60 days applies to late premium payments on the Exotic Pet plan. Before cancelling your Exotic Pet coverage, we send written notice at least 20 days in advance.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Definitions and Conditions

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
