# Velmora Assurance Multi-Pet Pet Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Multi-Pet pet plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Benefits

The annual benefit limit for Multi-Pet is $20,000. Multi-Pet reimburses 60% of eligible veterinary costs after the deductible. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Waiting Periods

Illness coverage under Multi-Pet begins after a 10 days waiting period. Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Eligibility

Multi-Pet accepts new enrollments for pets up to 6 years old. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Exclusions

The Multi-Pet plan does not cover:

- food and supplements unless prescribed
- breeding and pregnancy costs
- pre-existing conditions
- grooming

## General Terms

A grace period of 15 days applies to late premium payments on the Multi-Pet plan. The minimum cancellation notice for Multi-Pet is 30 days.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
