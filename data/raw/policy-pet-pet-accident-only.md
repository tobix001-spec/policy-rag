# Velmora Assurance Pet Accident Only Pet Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Pet Accident Only pet plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Benefits

Once the deductible is met, Pet Accident Only pays back 70% of covered vet bills. Pet Accident Only pays up to $10,000 in benefits per policy year. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Waiting Periods

Illness coverage under Pet Accident Only begins after a 15 days waiting period. We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Eligibility

The maximum enrollment age for Pet Accident Only is 8 years. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The Pet Accident Only plan does not cover:

- cosmetic procedures such as tail docking
- food and supplements unless prescribed
- grooming
- breeding and pregnancy costs

## General Terms

Pet Accident Only policyholders receive a 45 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. Before cancelling your Pet Accident Only coverage, we send written notice at least 45 days in advance.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Definitions and Conditions

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
