# Velmora Assurance Pet Complete Pet Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Pet Complete pet plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Benefits

Pet Complete reimburses 85% of eligible veterinary costs after the deductible. The annual benefit limit for Pet Complete is $7,500. Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Waiting Periods

Pet Complete has a 7 days waiting period for illnesses from the policy start date. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Eligibility

Pet Complete accepts new enrollments for pets up to 10 years old. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Exclusions

The Pet Complete plan does not cover:

- cosmetic procedures such as tail docking
- pre-existing conditions
- breeding and pregnancy costs
- grooming

## General Terms

A grace period of 10 days applies to late premium payments on the Pet Complete plan. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 14 days written notice before cancelling a Pet Complete plan.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Definitions and Conditions

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
