# Velmora Assurance Pet Wellness Pet Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Pet Wellness pet plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Benefits

The annual benefit limit for Pet Wellness is $5,000. Once the deductible is met, Pet Wellness pays back 90% of covered vet bills. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Waiting Periods

New Pet Wellness policies have a 21 days illness waiting period. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Eligibility

The maximum enrollment age for Pet Wellness is 14 years. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Exclusions

The Pet Wellness plan does not cover:

- breeding and pregnancy costs
- cosmetic procedures such as tail docking
- grooming
- pre-existing conditions

## General Terms

Pet Wellness policyholders receive a 20 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 60 days written notice before cancelling a Pet Wellness plan.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Definitions and Conditions

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
