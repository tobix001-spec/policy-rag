# Velmora Assurance Senior Pet Pet Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Senior Pet pet plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Benefits

Senior Pet pays up to $15,000 in benefits per policy year. Once the deductible is met, Senior Pet pays back 75% of covered vet bills. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Waiting Periods

Senior Pet has a 30 days waiting period for illnesses from the policy start date. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Eligibility

The maximum enrollment age for Senior Pet is 12 years. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The Senior Pet plan does not cover:

- grooming
- cosmetic procedures such as tail docking
- food and supplements unless prescribed
- breeding and pregnancy costs

## General Terms

A grace period of 31 days applies to late premium payments on the Senior Pet plan. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 10 days written notice before cancelling a Senior Pet plan.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
