# Velmora Assurance Annual Multi-Trip Travel Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Annual Multi-Trip travel plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Trip Protection

Annual Multi-Trip covers trip cancellation up to $25,000. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Medical Coverage

Annual Multi-Trip provides medical evacuation and repatriation coverage of up to $1,000,000. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Baggage

Under Annual Multi-Trip, a bag must be delayed at least 24 hours before the delay benefit applies. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Optional Upgrades

Annual Multi-Trip allows the cancel-for-any-reason upgrade only within 20 days of the initial deposit. No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Exclusions

The Annual Multi-Trip plan does not cover:

- travel against government advisories
- pandemic-related border closures
- lost cash
- extreme sports such as BASE jumping

## General Terms

If a Annual Multi-Trip premium is missed, coverage continues during a 31 days grace period. The minimum cancellation notice for Annual Multi-Trip is 14 days.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
