# Velmora Assurance Business Traveler Travel Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Business Traveler travel plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Trip Protection

Trip cancellation benefits under Business Traveler reimburse prepaid, non-refundable costs up to $2,500 per trip. No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

## Medical Coverage

The Business Traveler plan includes $100,000 in emergency evacuation benefits. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Baggage

Under Business Traveler, a bag must be delayed at least 12 hours before the delay benefit applies. Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Optional Upgrades

The cancel-for-any-reason upgrade for Business Traveler must be purchased within 15 days of the initial trip deposit. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Exclusions

The Business Traveler plan does not cover:

- pandemic-related border closures
- extreme sports such as BASE jumping
- pre-existing conditions without the waiver
- lost cash

## General Terms

Business Traveler policyholders receive a 10 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. The minimum cancellation notice for Business Traveler is 30 days.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Definitions and Conditions

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
