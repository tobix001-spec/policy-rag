# Velmora Assurance Student Abroad Travel Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Student Abroad travel plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Trip Protection

Student Abroad covers trip cancellation up to $10,000. Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Medical Coverage

Emergency medical evacuation is covered up to $250,000 under Student Abroad. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Baggage

Under Student Abroad, a bag must be delayed at least 18 hours before the delay benefit applies. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Optional Upgrades

The cancel-for-any-reason upgrade for Student Abroad must be purchased within 14 days of the initial trip deposit. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Exclusions

The Student Abroad plan does not cover:

- lost cash
- pandemic-related border closures
- extreme sports such as BASE jumping
- travel against government advisories

## General Terms

Student Abroad policyholders receive a 60 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 20 days written notice before cancelling a Student Abroad plan.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Definitions and Conditions

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
