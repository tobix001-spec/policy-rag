# Velmora Assurance Trip Lite Travel Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Trip Lite travel plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Trip Protection

Trip Lite covers trip cancellation up to $15,000. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Medical Coverage

Trip Lite provides medical evacuation and repatriation coverage of up to $750,000. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Baggage

Trip Lite reimburses essential purchases after a baggage delay of 8 hours. If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Optional Upgrades

To add cancel-for-any-reason coverage to Trip Lite, buy it within 7 days of your first trip payment. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Exclusions

The Trip Lite plan does not cover:

- pandemic-related border closures
- pre-existing conditions without the waiver
- extreme sports such as BASE jumping
- travel against government advisories

## General Terms

If a Trip Lite premium is missed, coverage continues during a 20 days grace period. Velmora Assurance will provide at least 60 days written notice before cancelling a Trip Lite plan.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

## Definitions and Conditions

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
