# Velmora Assurance Trip Premier Travel Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Trip Premier travel plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Trip Protection

Trip Premier covers trip cancellation up to $7,500. Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

## Medical Coverage

The Trip Premier plan includes $50,000 in emergency evacuation benefits. In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Baggage

Trip Premier reimburses essential purchases after a baggage delay of 6 hours. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Optional Upgrades

Trip Premier allows the cancel-for-any-reason upgrade only within 10 days of the initial deposit. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Exclusions

The Trip Premier plan does not cover:

- pre-existing conditions without the waiver
- travel against government advisories
- extreme sports such as BASE jumping
- pandemic-related border closures

## General Terms

Trip Premier policyholders receive a 45 days grace period before coverage lapses for non-payment. Before cancelling your Trip Premier coverage, we send written notice at least 45 days in advance.

Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

## Definitions and Conditions

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

Legal action against Velmora Assurance may not be brought unless there has been full compliance with all terms of this document.

We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
