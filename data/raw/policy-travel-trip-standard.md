# Velmora Assurance Trip Standard Travel Plan — Policy Document

## Overview

This document describes the Trip Standard travel plan offered by Velmora Assurance. It explains what is covered, what you pay, and the conditions that apply to your coverage.

Please read it carefully and keep it with your declarations page.

## Trip Protection

Trip Standard covers trip cancellation up to $5,000. Where other insurance applies to the same loss, we pay only our share of the loss in proportion to our limit.

## Medical Coverage

The Trip Standard plan includes $500,000 in emergency evacuation benefits. Subrogation: if we make a payment, we are entitled to recover it from any party responsible for the loss.

## Baggage

Trip Standard reimburses essential purchases after a baggage delay of 4 hours. Benefits are subject to all terms, conditions, limits and exclusions of this document, including any endorsements.

## Optional Upgrades

Trip Standard allows the cancel-for-any-reason upgrade only within 21 days of the initial deposit. We may examine any covered person, inspect damaged property, and request records reasonably needed to evaluate a claim.

## Exclusions

The Trip Standard plan does not cover:

- pre-existing conditions without the waiver
- extreme sports such as BASE jumping
- travel against government advisories
- lost cash

## General Terms

If a Trip Standard premium is missed, coverage continues during a 30 days grace period. The minimum cancellation notice for Trip Standard is 10 days.

Coverage is provided only for losses that occur during the policy period and only if the premium has been paid when due.

If you or anyone covered under this document conceals or misrepresents a material fact, coverage may be voided from inception.

## Definitions and Conditions

Any provision of this document that conflicts with the laws of the state in which it was issued is amended to conform to those laws.

No waiver or change of any provision of this document is valid unless made by written endorsement issued by Velmora Assurance.

In this document, "you" and "your" refer to the named insured shown in the declarations, and "we", "us" and "our" refer to Velmora Assurance.

## Fraud Warning

Any person who knowingly files a statement of claim containing false or misleading information may be guilty of insurance fraud. Velmora Assurance reports suspected fraud to the appropriate authorities.
