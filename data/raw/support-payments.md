# Premium Payments and Discounts

## Overview

This article explains premium payments and discounts.

## Details

A late fee of $15 is charged when a payment is received after the due date.

Payments can be made by bank transfer, debit card or credit card.

Payment confirmation emails are sent within one hour.

Enrolling in autopay earns a 3% discount on your premium.

Choosing paperless billing saves $2 per month.
