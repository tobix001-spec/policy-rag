# Uploading Claim Documents in the Mobile App

## Overview

This article explains uploading claim documents in the mobile app.

## Details

The mobile app accepts files up to 10 MB each; larger files should be sent through the web portal.

Uploads resume automatically if your connection drops.

The app compresses photos automatically when possible.
